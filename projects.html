Keeyask Generating Station

Hydroelectric generating station at Gull Rapids on the lower Nelson River in northern Manitoba. Rated at about 695 MW with seven generating units, it is developed by a partnership of Manitoba Hydro and four Manitoba First Nations.

Scope of Work
Fly forms and scaffolding
Role
Superintendent
Key Responsibilities
Scheduling, manpower coordination and materials management

Ridley Island Energy Export Facility

AltaGas energy export facility on Ridley Island at Prince Rupert, British Columbia, developed with Vopak. The facility is designed to export LPG, methanol and other bulk liquids, supported by rail, logistics and marine infrastructure alongside AltaGas’s existing propane export terminal.

Scope of Work
Concrete foundations
Role
Concrete Foundations Superintendent
Key Responsibilities
Scheduling, workforce coordination, subcontractor oversight, materials management and concrete construction supervision

Timber-frame construction
